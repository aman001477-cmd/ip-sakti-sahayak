BIODIVERSITY ACT, 2002 - COMPREHENSIVE GUIDE
Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change, Government of India

CHAPTER I: PRELIMINARY

Section 1: Short title, extent and commencement
(1) This Act may be called the Biodiversity Act, 2002.
(2) It extends to the whole of India.
(3) It shall come into force on such date as the Central Government may, by notification in the Official Gazette, appoint.

Section 2: Definitions
In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires:
(a) "Biodiversity" means the variability among living organisms from all sources including terrestrial, marine and other aquatic ecosystems and the ecological complexes of which they are part.
(b) "Biological resource" means plants, animals and micro-organisms or parts thereof, their genetic material and by-products.
(c) "Bio-survey and bio-utilization" means survey or collection of biological resources for purposes of research, bio-survey, bio-utilization or commercial utilization.
(d) "Centre" means the National Biodiversity Authority established under section 8.
(e) "Commercial utilization" means direct or indirect use of biological resources for commercial purposes.
(f) "Local Biodiversity Management Committee" means a committee established under section 41.
(g) "National Biodiversity Authority" means the authority established under section 8.
(h) "Non-transferable certificate of approval" means a certificate issued under section 21.
(i) "Person" includes an individual, Hindu undivided family, association, body corporate, whether registered or not, firm or company.
(j) "People's Biodiversity Register" means a register maintained under section 41.
(k) "Prescribed" means prescribed by rules made under this Act.
(l) "State Biodiversity Board" means a board established under section 22.
(m) "Sustainable use" means use of biological resources in such a manner that it does not lead to long-term ecological degradation.

CHAPTER II: National Biodiversity Authority

Section 3: Establishment of National Biodiversity Authority
(1) The Central Government shall, by notification, establish a body to be known as the National Biodiversity Authority.
(2) The Authority shall be a body corporate having perpetual succession and a common seal.
(3) The Authority shall have its headquarters in Chennai.

Section 4: Composition of Authority
(1) The Authority shall consist of:
(a) a Chairperson to be appointed by the Central Government;
(b) Chief Executive Officer;
(c) three ex-officio members representing Ministries of the Central Government;
(d) three ex-officio members representing State Governments;
(e) nine members to be nominated by the Central Government;
(f) two members representing NGOs;
(g) one member representing tribal communities;
(h) one member representing local bodies;
(i) one member representing farmers;
(j) one member representing women's organizations;
(k) one member representing industry.

Section 5: Term of office and conditions of service
(1) The term of office of the Chairperson and members shall be three years.
(2) The conditions of service shall be as prescribed.

Section 6: Disqualification for appointment
A person shall be disqualified for appointment if he:
(a) has been convicted of an offence;
(b) has been declared insolvent;
(c) is of unsound mind;
(d) holds any office of profit.

Section 7: Vacancies
The Authority may function notwithstanding any vacancy.

Section 8: Powers and functions of Authority
The Authority shall:
(a) advise the Central Government on matters relating to conservation of biological diversity;
(b) advise State Governments on matters relating to conservation of biological diversity;
(c) grant approvals for access to biological resources;
(d) grant approvals for intellectual property rights;
(e) advise on benefit sharing;
(f) compile and maintain data on biological resources;
(g) establish and maintain bioinformatics infrastructure;
(h) take such other measures as may be necessary.

Section 9: Meetings of Authority
(1) The Authority shall meet at least four times a year.
(2) The quorum shall be one-third of the total membership.
(3) The decisions shall be taken by majority vote.

Section 10: Committees of Authority
The Authority may constitute committees for:
(a) scientific matters;
(b) legal matters;
(c) financial matters;
(d) any other matter.

Section 11: Officers and servants of Authority
(1) The Authority may appoint such officers and servants as it considers necessary.
(2) The conditions of service shall be as prescribed.

Section 12: Delegation of powers
The Authority may delegate its powers to:
(a) the Chairperson;
(b) the Chief Executive Officer;
(c) any committee;
(d) any officer.

Section 13: Annual report
The Authority shall prepare an annual report and submit it to the Central Government.

Section 14: Accounts and audit
The accounts of the Authority shall be audited by the Comptroller and Auditor-General.

Section 15: Grant by Central Government
The Central Government may make grants to the Authority.

CHAPTER III: State Biodiversity Boards

Section 16: Establishment of State Biodiversity Boards
(1) The State Government shall, by notification, establish a board to be known as the State Biodiversity Board.
(2) The Board shall be a body corporate having perpetual succession and a common seal.

Section 17: Composition of Board
(1) The Board shall consist of:
(a) a Chairperson to be appointed by the State Government;
(b) Chief Executive Officer;
(c) three ex-officio members representing departments of the State Government;
(d) five members to be nominated by the State Government;
(e) two members representing NGOs;
(f) one member representing tribal communities;
(g) one member representing local bodies;
(h) one member representing farmers;
(i) one member representing women's organizations.

Section 18: Term of office and conditions of service
(1) The term of office of the Chairperson and members shall be three years.
(2) The conditions of service shall be as prescribed.

Section 19: Powers and functions of Board
The Board shall:
(a) advise the State Government on matters relating to conservation of biological diversity;
(b) grant approvals for access to biological resources within the State;
(c) grant approvals for intellectual property rights within the State;
(d) advise on benefit sharing within the State;
(e) compile and maintain data on biological resources within the State;
(f) take such other measures as may be necessary.

Section 20: Meetings of Board
(1) The Board shall meet at least four times a year.
(2) The quorum shall be one-third of the total membership.

Section 21: Officers and servants of Board
(1) The Board may appoint such officers and servants as it considers necessary.
(2) The conditions of service shall be as prescribed.

CHAPTER IV: Local Biodiversity Management Committees

Section 22: Constitution of Local Biodiversity Management Committees
(1) Every local body shall constitute a Local Biodiversity Management Committee.
(2) The Committee shall consist of:
(a) a Chairperson elected from among the members;
(b) seven members elected from among the inhabitants of the area;
(c) one member nominated by the State Biodiversity Board;
(d) one member representing women's organizations;
(e) one member representing tribal communities.

Section 23: Term of office
The term of office of the members shall be five years.

Section 24: Powers and functions of Committee
The Committee shall:
(a) prepare People's Biodiversity Register;
(b) advise on conservation of biological diversity;
(c) advise on sustainable use of biological resources;
(d) advise on benefit sharing;
(e) promote biodiversity conservation awareness;
(f) take such other measures as may be necessary.

Section 25: People's Biodiversity Register
(1) The Committee shall prepare and maintain a People's Biodiversity Register.
(2) The Register shall contain:
(a) details of biological resources;
(b) details of traditional knowledge;
(c) details of local communities;
(d) any other particulars as may be prescribed.

Section 26: Access to biological resources
No person shall access any biological resource without the approval of the Committee.

CHAPTER V: Regulation of Access to Biological Resources

Section 27: Application for access
(1) Any person desiring to access any biological resource shall make an application to the Authority or the Board.
(2) The application shall contain:
(a) description of the biological resource;
(b) purpose of access;
(c) duration of access;
(d) details of the applicant;
(e) any other particulars as may be prescribed.

Section 28: Consideration of application
The Authority or the Board shall consider the application and may:
(a) grant approval;
(b) refuse approval;
(c) impose conditions;
(d) require further information.

Section 29: Terms and conditions
The approval shall be subject to:
(a) payment of access fee;
(b) benefit sharing;
(c) compliance with conditions;
(d) any other terms as may be prescribed.

Section 30: Benefit sharing
(1) The applicant shall share benefits with:
(a) the local communities;
(b) the State Biodiversity Board;
(c) the National Biodiversity Authority.
(2) The benefit sharing shall be as prescribed.

Section 31: Non-transferable certificate of approval
(1) The Authority may issue a non-transferable certificate of approval.
(2) The certificate shall be valid for such period as may be prescribed.
(3) The certificate shall not be transferred without the approval of the Authority.

CHAPTER VI: Intellectual Property Rights

Section 32: Application for intellectual property rights
(1) Any person desiring to obtain intellectual property rights based on biological resources shall make an application to the Authority.
(2) The application shall contain:
(a) description of the invention;
(b) details of the biological resources used;
(c) details of the applicant;
(d) any other particulars as may be prescribed.

Section 33: Consideration of application
The Authority shall consider the application and may:
(a) grant approval;
(b) refuse approval;
(c) impose conditions;
(d) require further information.

Section 34: Terms and conditions
The approval shall be subject to:
(a) payment of fee;
(b) benefit sharing;
(c) compliance with conditions;
(d) any other terms as may be prescribed.

Section 35: Prior approval of State Biodiversity Board
(1) No person shall apply for intellectual property rights without the prior approval of the State Biodiversity Board.
(2) The State Biodiversity Board shall consider the application and may:
(a) grant approval;
(b) refuse approval;
(c) impose conditions;
(d) require further information.

Section 36: Prior approval of Local Biodiversity Management Committee
(1) No person shall access any biological resource without the prior approval of the Local Biodiversity Management Committee.
(2) The Committee shall consider the application and may:
(a) grant approval;
(b) refuse approval;
(c) impose conditions;
(d) require further information.

CHAPTER VII: Exemptions

Section 37: Exemption for local communities
(1) Local communities shall be exempt from the provisions of this Act for:
(a) subsistence use;
(b) traditional practices;
(c) exchange of materials;
(d) any other use as may be prescribed.

Section 38: Exemption for research
(1) Researchers shall be exempt from the provisions of this Act for:
(a) basic research;
(b) taxonomy;
(c) academic research;
(d) any other research as may be prescribed.

Section 39: Exemption for exchange
(1) Exchange of biological resources shall be exempt from the provisions of this Act for:
(a) traditional practices;
(b) local use;
(c) any other exchange as may be prescribed.

CHAPTER VIII: National Biodiversity Fund

Section 40: Establishment of National Biodiversity Fund
(1) The Central Government shall establish a fund to be known as the National Biodiversity Fund.
(2) The Fund shall be utilized for:
(a) conservation of biological diversity;
(b) sustainable use of biological resources;
(c) benefit sharing;
(d) any other purpose as may be prescribed.

Section 41: State Biodiversity Fund
(1) The State Government shall establish a fund to be known as the State Biodiversity Fund.
(2) The Fund shall be utilized for:
(a) conservation of biological diversity;
(b) sustainable use of biological resources;
(c) benefit sharing;
(d) any other purpose as may be prescribed.

CHAPTER IX: People's Biodiversity Register

Section 42: Maintenance of People's Biodiversity Register
(1) The Local Biodiversity Management Committee shall prepare and maintain a People's Biodiversity Register.
(2) The Register shall be maintained in the local language.
(3) The Register shall be open to public inspection.

Section 43: Contents of People's Biodiversity Register
The Register shall contain:
(a) details of biological resources;
(b) details of traditional knowledge;
(c) details of local communities;
(d) details of sacred groves;
(e) details of traditional practices;
(f) any other particulars as may be prescribed.

Section 44: Access to People's Biodiversity Register
(1) The Register shall be open to public inspection.
(2) Any person may, on payment of the prescribed fee, obtain a copy of any entry in the Register.

CHAPTER X: Biodiversity Heritage Sites

Section 45: Declaration of Biodiversity Heritage Sites
(1) The State Government may declare any area as a Biodiversity Heritage Site.
(2) The declaration shall be made in the Official Gazette.
(3) The declaration shall specify:
(a) the boundaries of the site;
(b) the biological resources to be protected;
(c) any other particulars as may be prescribed.

Section 46: Management of Biodiversity Heritage Sites
(1) The State Biodiversity Board shall manage the Biodiversity Heritage Sites.
(2) The Board may prepare a management plan for the site.
(3) The management plan shall specify:
(a) the objectives of management;
(b) the measures to be taken;
(c) the institutions to be involved;
(d) any other particulars as may be prescribed.

Section 47: Rights of local communities
(1) The local communities shall have the right to:
(a) access the site;
(b) collect biological resources;
(c) use biological resources;
(d) share benefits.
(2) The rights shall be subject to the management plan.

CHAPTER XI: Offences and Penalties

Section 48: Offences
The following are offences:
(a) accessing biological resources without approval;
(b) applying for intellectual property rights without approval;
(c) failing to share benefits;
(d) providing false information;
(e) obstructing any person in the discharge of his duties;
(f) any other offence as may be prescribed.

Section 49: Penalties
The penalties for offences are:
(a) accessing biological resources without approval: imprisonment up to 5 years or fine up to Rs. 10 lakhs;
(b) applying for intellectual property rights without approval: imprisonment up to 3 years or fine up to Rs. 5 lakhs;
(c) failing to share benefits: imprisonment up to 2 years or fine up to Rs. 2 lakhs;
(d) providing false information: imprisonment up to 1 year or fine up to Rs. 1 lakh.

Section 50: Cognizance of offences
(1) No court shall take cognizance of an offence except on a complaint made by:
(a) the National Biodiversity Authority;
(b) the State Biodiversity Board;
(c) the Local Biodiversity Management Committee;
(d) any person interested.

Section 51: Power to compound offences
The Authority may compound any offence.

Section 52: Protection of action taken in good faith
No suit or prosecution shall lie against any person for anything done in good faith under this Act.

CHAPTER XII: Miscellaneous

Section 53: Power to make rules
(1) The Central Government may make rules for carrying out the provisions of this Act.
(2) The rules may provide for:
(a) the forms;
(b) the fees;
(c) the procedure;
(d) the time limits;
(e) any other matter as may be prescribed.

Section 54: Power to make regulations
(1) The Authority may make regulations for the efficient conduct of business.
(2) The regulations may provide for:
(a) the procedure;
(b) the forms;
(c) the fees;
(d) any other matter as may be prescribed.

Section 55: Bar of jurisdiction of courts
No court shall have jurisdiction to question the validity of any rule or regulation.

Section 56: Transitional provisions
(1) The provisions of this Act shall apply to access of biological resources on or after the commencement of this Act.
(2) Any access of biological resources before the commencement of this Act shall be governed by the provisions of this Act.

Section 57: Repeal and saving
(1) The Biological Diversity Act, 2002 is hereby enacted.
(2) Anything done or any action taken under any previous law shall be deemed to have been done or taken under this Act.

APPENDIX A: BENEFIT SHARING MECHANISM

1. Access Fee: Paid by the applicant to the Authority/Board
2. Royalty: Paid by the applicant to the local communities
3. Joint Research: Collaborative research with local communities
4. Capacity Building: Training and skill development
5. Technology Transfer: Transfer of technology to local communities
6. Revenue Sharing: Sharing of revenue from commercialization

APPENDIX B: TYPES OF BIOLOGICAL RESOURCES

1. Plants: Including medicinal plants, aromatic plants, crop plants
2. Animals: Including livestock, poultry, fish
3. Micro-organisms: Including bacteria, fungi, viruses
4. Genetic Material: Including DNA, RNA, genes
5. By-products: Including extracts, metabolites
6. Traditional Knowledge: Including folk medicine, traditional practices

APPENDIX C: ACCESS PROCEDURE

Step 1: Application
1. Submit application to Authority/Board
2. Pay prescribed fee
3. Provide required information

Step 2: Consideration
1. Authority/Board considers application
2. May require additional information
3. May impose conditions

Step 3: Approval
1. If approved, issue certificate
2. Specify terms and conditions
3. Specify benefit sharing mechanism

Step 4: Compliance
1. Comply with terms and conditions
2. Share benefits as prescribed
3. Submit reports as required

APPENDIX D: BENEFIT SHARING MECHANISM

1. Access Fee: 2-5% of the value of biological resources
2. Royalty: 1-3% of the revenue from commercialization
3. Joint Research: 10-20% of the research budget
4. Capacity Building: 5-10% of the project cost
5. Technology Transfer: As agreed between parties
6. Revenue Sharing: 10-30% of the revenue from commercialization
