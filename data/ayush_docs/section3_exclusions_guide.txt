SECTION 3 EXCLUSIONS — COMPLETE WALKTHROUGH (CLAUSES a–p)
Indian Patents Act, 1970 (as amended; clause (g) omitted in 2002)

Section 3 lists subject matter that is NOT an invention. Authoritative letters verified against the
bare Act (see also patents_act_2024.pdf in this knowledge base):

(a) FRIVOLOUS / CONTRARY TO NATURAL LAW — e.g., perpetual-motion machines.
(b) PUBLIC ORDER / MORALITY / HARM — inventions whose exploitation would harm human, animal or
    plant life, health or the environment (e.g., bio-weapons, human cloning processes).
(c) SCIENTIFIC PRINCIPLE / ABSTRACT THEORY / NATURAL DISCOVERY — e.g., laws of physics, newly
    discovered micro-organisms or substances as found in nature.
(d) NEW FORM WITHOUT ENHANCED EFFICACY — salts, polymorphs, isomers etc. of known substances are
    the SAME substance unless significantly enhanced THERAPEUTIC efficacy is shown. The
    anti-evergreening provision (see novartis_glivec_case.txt).
(e) MERE ADMIXTURE — a substance obtained by mixing known components with only aggregation of
    properties and no synergy (e.g., a simple physical mixture of two known drugs with no
    unexpected combined effect).
(f) MERE ARRANGEMENT / DUPLICATION — re-arranging known devices each working independently in a
    known way (e.g., bolting a known fan to a known heater with no interaction).
(g) OMITTED by the Patents (Amendment) Act, 2002 — no longer in force.
(h) AGRICULTURE / HORTICULTURE METHODS — e.g., a method of pruning or cultivating crops.
(i) MEDICAL TREATMENT / DIAGNOSIS — surgical, curative, prophylactic, diagnostic or therapeutic
    treatment of humans or animals. NOTE: the DRUGS, devices and diagnostic KITS themselves remain
    patentable as products; only the treatment METHOD is barred.
(j) PLANTS / ANIMALS / SEEDS / VARIETIES / ESSENTIALLY BIOLOGICAL PROCESSES — micro-organisms
    are expressly EXCEPTED and can be patented. Plant varieties are protected instead under the
    PPV&FR Act, 2001 (sui generis system).
(k) MATHEMATICAL / BUSINESS METHOD / COMPUTER PROGRAMME PER SE / ALGORITHMS — software as such
    is barred, but computer-related inventions with a technical effect or technical contribution
    may be patentable (see software_patents_cri_cases.txt; CRI Guidelines 2017/2025).
(l) LITERARY / ARTISTIC WORKS — books, music, films (protected by the Copyright Act, 1957).
(m) MENTAL ACTS / GAMES — schemes, rules, methods of playing games.
(n) PRESENTATION OF INFORMATION — e.g., timetables, charts, raw data compilations as such.
(o) TOPOGRAPHY OF INTEGRATED CIRCUITS — protected separately under the Semiconductor Integrated
    Circuits Layout-Design Act, 2000.
(p) TRADITIONAL KNOWLEDGE — inventions which in effect are TK or mere aggregation/duplication of
    known properties of traditionally known components (see tkdl and neem/turmeric cases).

EXAMINATION TIP (IPO TK Guidelines 2012): TK/biological-material applications must be examined
particularly under clauses (c), (e), (i), (j) and (p), with Form-1 NBA-declaration compliance.
