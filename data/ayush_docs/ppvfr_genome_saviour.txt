PLANT GENOME SAVIOUR AWARDS & FARMERS' VARIETY SUCCESS STORIES
PPV&FR Authority | Agro-biodiversity Conservation Incentives

1. THE AWARD SYSTEM
The Protection of Plant Varieties and Farmers' Rights Authority confers the annual Plant Genome
Saviour Community Awards (plus Farmer Rewards and Recognitions) on farming and tribal
communities that conserve landraces, wild relatives and traditional varieties and share them as
gene donors for breeding. Awards carry cash prizes and citations and are widely publicised to
encourage on-farm conservation.

2. WHY IT MATTERS FOR ABS
Conservation awards complete the incentive loop: communities that maintain the genetic
reservoir get public recognition and money; breeders that use the material share benefits under
Section 26; the National Gene Fund channels resources back into conservation.

3. FARMERS' VARIETIES ON RECORD
The Authority registers extant and farmers' varieties (traditionally cultivated varieties,
including tribal selections) with adapted DUS examination — giving grassroots bred material the
same certificate status as institutional varieties. Registered farmers' varieties fetch
recognition, licensing interest and Gene Fund linkages.

4. LINK TO AYUSH AND TK
Several conserved landraces are medicinal/aromatic plants feeding Ayurvedic supply chains;
documentation in People's Biodiversity Registers plus PPVFR registration creates layered
defensive + positive protection alongside TKDL and GI tags.
