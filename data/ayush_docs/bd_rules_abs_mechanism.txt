BIODIVERSITY RULES 2004 & ABS MECHANISM — HOW APPROVALS ACTUALLY WORK
Biological Diversity Rules, 2004 | NBA ABS Regulations, 2014 | 2023 Interface

1. THE THREE-TIER MACHINERY
(a) NATIONAL BIODIVERSITY AUTHORITY (NBA), Chennai — approvals for foreign entities/persons,
    IPR applications based on Indian biological resources, and third-party transfers of
    research results.
(b) STATE BIODIVERSITY BOARDS (SBBs) — approvals for domestic commercial utilisation and
    bio-survey within the state.
(c) BIODIVERSITY MANAGEMENT COMMITTEES (BMCs) — local-body committees maintaining People's
    Biodiversity Registers (PBRs) and consulted on local access.

2. APPROVAL ROUTES (RULES, 2004)
Prescribed application forms exist for: access for research/commercial use (foreigners),
transfer of research results, and IPR applications (Form III category). NBA must decide IPR
permission applications within a statutory timeline (ninety days under the original scheme);
SBBs operate parallel state windows.

3. THE 2014 ABS REGULATIONS — MONEY MECHANISM
The Guidelines on Access to Biological Resources and Associated Knowledge and Benefits Sharing
Regulations, 2014 introduced a graded, turnover-linked benefit-sharing framework: traders pay a
smaller fraction while manufacturers pay a higher share of annual gross ex-factory sale value
of the biological resource (exact slabs are notified in the Regulations and updated guidance —
always verify the current Gazette text before advising a client). Collectors and local
communities receive shares through BMC-linked community funds.

4. IPR + NBA CLEARANCE CHECKLIST
(a) Patent Form-1 declaration on use of Indian biological material.
(b) NBA permission route BEFORE grant (original scheme) — track current post-2023-amendment
    procedures, which simplified domestic and AYUSH-adjacent pathways.
(c) Benefit-sharing terms or exemption basis recorded in the file.

5. 2023 AMENDMENT INTERFACE
Decriminalisation (penalties adjudicated, NGT appeal), AYUSH-practitioner and cultivated-plant
relief, and lighter domestic intimation routes — see bd_amendment_2023.txt. Core foreign-access
and IPR-clearance discipline continues.
