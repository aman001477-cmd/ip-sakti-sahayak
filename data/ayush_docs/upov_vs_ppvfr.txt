UPOV vs INDIA'S PPV&FR ACT — WHY INDIA CHOSE ITS OWN SYSTEM
TRIPS Article 27.3(b) | Sui Generis Plant Variety Protection

1. THE TRIPS OBLIGATION
TRIPS Article 27.3(b) lets members exclude plants/animals from patents but REQUIRES an
"effective sui generis system" for plant varieties — via patents, a sui generis system, or a
combination. Two global models emerged: UPOV membership or a home-grown law. India chose its
own law: the PPV&FR Act, 2001.

2. UPOV IN BRIEF
The International Union for the Protection of New Varieties of Plants (UPOV Convention:
1961, revised 1978 and 1991) grants breeders' rights on DUS-tested varieties (Distinctness,
Uniformity, Stability) plus novelty.
(a) UPOV 1978: contained a broad "farmers' privilege" to save and replant seed.
(b) UPOV 1991: narrowed the privilege to an OPTIONAL exception for farm-saved seed "within
    reasonable limits", added essentially derived varieties (EDV), extended terms (20/25 years).

3. INDIA'S PPV&FR ACT — FARMER-FIRST DIFFERENCES
(a) FARMERS AS BREEDERS: A farmer who breeds a new variety gets the SAME registration rights as
    a professional breeder (Section 39) — unheard of under UPOV.
(b) RIGHT TO SAVE, USE, SOW, RESOW, EXCHANGE AND SHARE seed (only branded-seed sale of
    protected varieties is barred).
(c) COMPENSATION FOR CROP FAILURE where a breeder's disclosed performance claims fail.
(d) BENEFIT SHARING (Section 26) with farming/tribal communities plus the National Gene Fund
    (Section 45) for conservation rewards.
(e) EXTANT AND FARMERS' VARIETIES registrable with relaxed DUS application.

4. WHY NOT UPOV 1991?
Parliament and farm movements held that UPOV 1991's narrowed farmers' privilege and EDV-heavy
breeder rights would hurt India's 100+ million smallholders and seed sovereignty; the PPV&FR
Act is widely cited as the world's most farmer-friendly sui generis system and is TRIPS-
compliant without UPOV membership.

5. KEYWORDS
UPOV 1978, UPOV 1991, sui generis, DUS, farmers privilege, EDV, gene fund, benefit sharing,
Section 39, seed sovereignty, TRIPS 27.3(b).
