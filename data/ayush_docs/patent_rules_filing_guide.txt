INDIAN PATENT FILING & PROSECUTION — PRACTICAL GUIDE
Patents Act, 1970 with the Patent (Amendment) Rules, 2024

1. PRE-FILING
(a) PRIOR-ART SEARCH: Search Indian Patent Office (InPASSA), WIPO Patentscope, Google Patents and
    TKDL for traditional-knowledge overlaps before drafting.
(b) DECIDE PROVISIONAL vs COMPLETE: File a provisional specification (Form 2) to secure a date,
    then the complete specification within 12 months.
(c) FOREIGN FILING PERMISSION (Section 39): If all inventors are resident in India, either file
    first in India or obtain written permission (Form 25) before filing abroad.

2. KEY FORMS
- Form 1: Application for grant of patent.
- Form 2: Provisional / complete specification.
- Form 3: Statement and undertaking on corresponding foreign applications (Section 8) — must be
  kept updated during prosecution.
- Form 5: Declaration of inventorship.
- Form 18 / 18A: Request for examination (ordinary / expedited).
- Form 26: Power of attorney for patent agents.
- Form 27: Annual working statement (now once every THREE financial years under the 2024 Rules).
- Form 30: Miscellaneous petitions including timeline extensions.

3. TIMELINES (POST-2024 RULES)
(a) PUBLICATION: Automatic at 18 months from priority/filing; early publication on request (Form 9).
(b) REQUEST FOR EXAMINATION: Within THIRTY-ONE MONTHS from priority date or filing date,
    whichever is earlier (reduced from 48 months by the 2024 amendment).
(c) FIRST EXAMINATION REPORT (FER): Reply with amendments/arguments within the prescribed period
    (extendable on petition with fees); hearings may be held by the Controller.
(d) GRANT: On meeting all requirements; term is 20 years from the international/date of filing.
(e) RENEWALS: Annual renewal fees from the 3rd year onward to keep the patent in force.

4. OPPOSITIONS
(a) PRE-GRANT REPRESENTATION (Section 25(1)): Any person may oppose after publication but before
    grant on grounds including wrongful obtainment, anticipation, obviousness and Section 3 bars —
    heavily used by generic companies and NGOs in pharma cases.
(b) POST-GRANT OPPOSITION (Section 25(2)): Within 12 months of grant publication before an
    Opposition Board; grounds include Section 3(d)/3(p) and traditional-knowledge bars.

5. 2024 RULES — HEADLINE CHANGES
(a) RFE window shortened to 31 months (faster prosecution).
(b) Form 27 working statements consolidated to once every three financial years.
(c) Streamlined extension-of-time practice with official fees; Controller empowered to condone
    delays in most timelines.
(d) Clearer divisional-application practice and revised patent-agent examination framework.
(e) Jan Vishwas Act, 2023 decriminalised several minor procedural defaults into monetary
    penalties adjudicated by designated officers.

6. COST INDICATORS (VERIFY CURRENT SCHEDULE I BEFORE FILING)
Fees are slab-based: lowest for individuals/startups/small entities, higher for large entities;
foreign applicants pay in USD. Budget heads: filing + publication + examination + hearing +
grant + annual renewals + agent charges.

post-grant opposition, Section 25, Section 39, foreign filing permission, 2024 Rules, working
